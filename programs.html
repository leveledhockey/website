Power Edge Pro

Power Edge Pro is a certified, science-backed training system used by elite programs across North America — now available in North Vancouver. Sessions are built around the Power Edge Pro reactive countering apparatus to develop explosive edge work, puck protection, and stickhandling under simulated game-pressure conditions.

Develop explosive crossovers and edge transitions
Build puck control under reactive, game-like pressure
Improve balance, agility, and on-ice quickness
Small groups ensure maximum reps and individual attention
Suitable for all skill levels — beginner through elite

Development Sessions

Our Development Sessions are structured on-ice training blocks covering every foundational element of the game. From skating mechanics to shot release, these sessions are designed to build complete, confident hockey players who perform when it matters most. Curriculum is adjusted based on the skill level of each group.

Skating mechanics — stride, crossovers, backwards skating
Stickhandling — in-tight, wide-area, and under pressure
Shooting — wrist shot, snap shot, one-timer mechanics
Puck support, positioning, and hockey sense
Game-scenario drills that translate directly to competition

Frequently Asked

We welcome players of all ages and skill levels — from beginners just getting comfortable on skates to competitive rep players preparing for tryouts. Programs and expectations are adjusted based on who's in each group.
Players should arrive in full hockey gear: skates, helmet with cage or visor, shoulder pads, elbow pads, gloves, shin guards, and hockey pants. Don't forget a stick and a water bottle. If you're unsure about anything, feel free to contact us ahead of your first session.
We intentionally keep group sizes small — typically 6 to 10 players per session — so that every athlete gets quality coaching time, real feedback, and enough reps to make each session count. We are not a factory-style hockey camp.
