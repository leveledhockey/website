Training Programs

Group

Power Edge Pro

Led by a Certified Level 2 PEP Instructor, this program builds elite edge control, puck handling, and decision-making through structured movement patterns that simulate real-game pressure. Players get significantly more puck touches and reps than traditional practices.

$55 / player / hr
Register
Group

Overspeed Power Skating

One of the only programs in BC partnered with Bronko Hockey, this session combines power skating with resistance training using the Bronko weighted belt system. Build stride strength, acceleration, and balance that translates directly to speed when the resistance comes off.

$55 / player / hr
Register
Group

Puck Skills

Develops offensive puck skills in motion and under pressure. Stick-handling in tight areas, puck protection, shooting, passing, and deception to create space. Every rep is designed to mirror what players face in real game situations.

$55 / player / hr
Register
Group

Battle Camp

Bridges skill development and in-game performance through small-area competitive scenarios: 1v1, 2v2, and 3v3 situations where players must win puck battles, make quick decisions, and execute under pressure.

$55 / player / hr
Register
Group

Defense Camp

A review of defensive fundamentals: defense-specific skating, breakouts, zone coverage, offensive activation, and 1-on-1 scenarios. Builds the tools and confidence to play a reliable, complete game from the back end.
