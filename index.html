What We Offer

Training Programs

Every program is built to push players further — technically, physically, and mentally. Choose the format that fits your game.

Group

Power Edge Pro

Led by a Certified Level 2 PEP Instructor, this program builds elite edge control, puck handling, and decision-making through structured movement patterns that simulate real-game pressure. Players get significantly more puck touches and reps than traditional practices.

$55 / player / hr

Group

Overspeed Power Skating

One of the only programs in BC partnered with Bronko Hockey, this session combines power skating with resistance training using the Bronko weighted belt system — building stride strength, acceleration, and balance that translates directly to speed when the resistance comes off.

$55 / player / hr

Group

Puck Skills

Develops offensive puck skills in motion and under pressure — stickhandling in tight areas, puck protection, shooting, passing, and deception to create space. Every rep is designed to mirror what players face in real game situations.

$55 / player / hr
Register
Group

Battle Camp

Bridges skill development and in-game performance through small-area competitive scenarios — 1v1, 2v2, and 3v3 situations where players must win puck battles, make quick decisions, and execute under pressure. Skill meets competition meets game.

$55 / player / hr

Group

Defense Camp

A thorough review of defensive fundamentals for players serious about the position — defense-specific skating, breakouts, zone coverage, offensive activation, and 1-on-1 scenarios. Builds the tools and confidence to play a reliable, complete game from the back end.
